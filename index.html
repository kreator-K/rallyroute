What “acts on your behalf” means

1. Understand intent

Origin, destination, departure time, mode, walking tolerance, and accessibility preferences.

2. Monitor trusted sources

Official restrictions first; public reports are confidence-scored and never treated as legal closures automatically.

3. Assess personal impact

Only alert when a disruption intersects the user's planned journey or stated preferences.

4. Adapt with consent

Recalculate the in-app recommendation automatically when enabled; ask for a decision when confidence is low or a trade-off is material.
